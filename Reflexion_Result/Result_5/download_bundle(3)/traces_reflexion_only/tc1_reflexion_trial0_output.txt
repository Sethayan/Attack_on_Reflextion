### Morning
- **Morning Coffee Plantation Tour at Bassi Estate (Coorg)**: Visit the Bassi Estate for a detailed tour of a coffee plantation, learn about the cultivation process, and taste the local coffee.
- **Activity at Madikeri Fort**: Take a guided visit to Madikeri Fort, a historical site nestled on a hill. Enjoy the historical significance and panoramic views of the surrounding countryside.

### Afternoon
- **Afternoon Hike at Kote Cheruvu Palace Museum (Coorg)**: Hike through the nearby hills and visit the Kote Cheruvu Palace Museum to gain insights into Coorg's rich history and traditions.
- **Visiting St. Philomena Church (Coorg)**: Explore the St. Philomena Church, a cultural and religious landmark that provides a deep dive into the local heritage.

### Evening
- **Evening Cultural Show at Madikeri Folk Museum**: Attend a cultural show at the Madikeri Folk Museum, featuring traditional dances and music performances.
