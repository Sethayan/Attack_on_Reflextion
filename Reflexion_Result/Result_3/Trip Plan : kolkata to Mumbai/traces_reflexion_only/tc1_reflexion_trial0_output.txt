## Day 1: Arrival and Exploration of the Sea Beach (September 1, 2026)
### Morning: 
- **Arrival at Lisbon Airport**
- **Departure to Praia de Estoril** (approximately 1.5 hours by car)
  - Visit the fine, calm waters of this popular beach, known for its relaxation and leisure.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Sunglasses, swimwear, sunscreen, beach towels, and snacks.
  - **Budget Friendly**: €5-€10 per person for parking and snacks.

### Afternoon: 
- **Visit to Praia da Torreira** in Sesimbra (approximately 1.5-2 hours by car)
  - Relax in the less crowded, serene atmosphere.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Sunscreen, swimwear, and a picnic basket.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Free entry, €5-€10 per person for snacks and drinks.

### Evening: 
- **Explore the Praia da Mina** in Cascais (approx. 30 minutes by car from Torreira) and enjoy the local nightlife at Bairro Alto with live music performances.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Comfortable shoes, a sweater, and items for a night out.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Meals from €10-€20 per person, entrance to Casa da Música for €15-€20.

## Day 2: Lisbon's Beaches and Cultural Integration (September 2, 2026)
### Morning: 
- **Explore Cais do Sodré** for its views of Lisbon's skyline and smaller, coastal beaches.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Sunscreen, sunglasses, a swimsuit, and light lunch.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Free beach access, €5-€10 for a light lunch.

### Afternoon: 
- **Visit to Praia dos Pescadores** and Praia da Mina in Cascais (approx. 20 minutes by car from Praia da Torreira)
  - Enjoy the beautiful, natural setting of these beaches.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Snacks, sunscreen, and comfortable shoes.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Free beach access, €5-€10 for drinks and snacks.

### Evening: 
- **Experience the local nightlife in Bairro Alto**
  - Highlights include Casa da Música and traditional bars.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Footwear suitable for walking, comfortable clothing, and items for dining out.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Meals and drinks from €10-€20 per person.

## Day 3: Alcântara Beach Day - Fun and Sun (September 3, 2026)
### Morning: 
- **Visit Alcântara Beach in Cascais** for excellent surfing and windsurfing conditions.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Swimsuit, sunscreen, and a beach bag.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Free beach access, €5-€10 for parking and snacks.

### Afternoon: 
- **Explore Monte da Caparica** park for its beachfront views and amenities.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Towel, a light lunch, and a camera for photos.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Free park access, €10-€20 for parking.

### Evening: 
- **Relax on the Tagus promenade in Belém** before dining at a riverside restaurant.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Comfortable clothing and light dinner items.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Dinner from €20-€30 per person, with views of the Lisbon skyline.

## Day 4: Coastal Exploration and Evening Cultural Experience (September 4, 2026)
### Morning and Afternoon: 
- **Day tripping to Peniche**
  - Visit the picturesque white houses and wild coast beaches of Peniche.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Sunglasses, swimwear, and a light lunch.
  - **Budget Friendly**: €10-€20 for parking and snacks, free beach access.

### Evening: 
- **Explore the Belém district**
  - Visit the Torre de Belém and Jerónimos Monastery.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Comfortable shoes for walking and a light dinner.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Admission to the Torre de Belém costs about €9-€20, dinner from €10-€30 per person.

## Day 5: Lisbon Nightlife and Departure (September 5, 2026)
### Morning: 
- **Visit Nazaré beach**
  - Known for its surfing culture and dramatic coastline.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Swap out swimwear and bring a light lunch.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Free beach access, €5-€10 for parking and snacks.

### Afternoon: 
- **Explore Rua da Flores and Pragal for a final sunset stroll and seafood dinner.**
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Itemized luggage and comfortable shoes for walking.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Walking around and a seafood dinner from €20-€40 per person.

### Evening: 
- **Conclude your trip in Lisbon with a Fado house experience**
  - Visit Casa da Musica or another traditional location.
  - **Packing Suggestions**: Comfortable clothing for cultural experience.
  - **Budget Friendly**: Admission for live Fado music from €20-€40, dinner from €20-€40 per person.

- **Budget-friendly**: €30-€40 per night
- **Luxury**: €100+ per night

- **Late-night drinks and dining**: €10-€20 per meal

- **Cost per activity**: €5-€10 per activity, with free options available

- **Travel pass**: €30-€50 for the week, based on local transport options

### Total Estimated Cost: €500-€1000 per person for the 5-day trip
