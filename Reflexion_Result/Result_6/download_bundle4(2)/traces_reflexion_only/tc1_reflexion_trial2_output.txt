## Day 2: Cultural and Historical Exploration
### Weather Conditions
- **Morning**: Sunny, temperatures around 23°C to 26°C.
- **Afternoon**: Partly cloudy, 25°C to 28°C.
- **Evening**: Mildly cool, 18°C to 20°C.

### Packing Suggestions
- Layered clothing for cooler evenings
- Light jacket
- Comfortable walking shoes

### Activities

## Day 3: Wildlife and Coffee Plantations
### Weather Conditions
- **Morning**: Moderate sunshine, temperatures around 22°C to 25°C.
- **Afternoon**: Cloudy, 24°C to 27°C.
- **Evening**: Cool, 18°C to 20°C.

### Packing Suggestions
- Raincoat (as there are occasional showers)
- Trekking shoes for hiking
- Binoculars for wildlife watching

### Activities
- **Morning**: Visit the Kodikeriyur Coffee Estate for a coffee plantation tour.
- **Afternoon**: Drive to Kudremukh National Park for a day trip to spot wildlife.
- **Evening**: Return to the hotel and relax.

### Evening
- **Dinner at Sarada Vilas Palace**: Enjoy a traditional Coorgi meal and learn about the cultural history of the region.
