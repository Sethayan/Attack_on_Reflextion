## Day 1: November 5, Travel Arrivals and Initial Exploration

### Morning
- **Arrival in Wayanad:**
  - Transfer from the airport to **Hotel Ananda Estate**, a charming resort that offers breathtaking views and cozy accommodation. The hotel is known for its proximity to major coffee plantations and wildlife reserves.
- **Morning Activity:**
  - **Visit Coffee Plantation Tour:**
    - **Gogaba Coffee Plantation Tour**: Start your day early with a guided tour of Gogaba Coffee Plantation, where you can learn about the coffee cultivation process, participate in a tasting session, and enjoy a scenic walk through the lush gardens.
    - **Local Breakfast:** Enjoy a traditional Coorgi breakfast at a nearby **Native Kitchen**. The menu includes dishes like Bisi Bele Bath, Coorgi Chicken, and Coconut Rice.

### Afternoon
- **Activity:**
  - **Visit Madikeri Fort:**
    - **Historical Site**: Madikeri Fort, an iconic structure with rich historical significance, provides panoramic views over the town below. The fort is a must-visit for those interested in Coorg's history and culture.
  - **Local Walk:**
    - Explore the **Nelum Thota Lake** (also known as Madikeri Reservoir), a peaceful spot for a leisurely walk and a great place to watch the morning sun and local birds.

### Evening
- **Locally Sourced Dinner:**
  - **Kodava Kitchens**: Dine at this restaurant known for its authentic Coorgi cuisine, featuring Kappa (rice prepared in bamboo) and other local delicacies prepared in a stone pan (Kambala).

### Day 2: November 6, Nature and Wildlife Exploration

### Morning
- **Morning Activity:**
  - **Visit Biligiri Rangan Hills:**
    - Venture into the Biligiri Rangan Hills, a biodiversity hotspot and a wildlife reservation. The trek to Kudremukh and the surrounding trails offer a chance to spot elephants, leopards, and a diverse array of bird life. 
  - **Morning Snack and Picnic Lunch:**
    - Pack a light snack at **Kudremukh Picnic Area**, a great spot to have a leisurely meal with views of the surrounding hills.

### Afternoon
- **Activity:**
  - **Dubare Elephant Camp:**
    - Spend a few hours at **Dubare Elephant Camp**, where you can ride elephants, take a guided walk with them, and learn more about their daily lives. This is a unique and memorable experience in a natural setting.
  - **Local Relaxation:**
    - Return to the **Hotel Ananda Estate** for a well-deserved rest, taking advantage of the resort amenities.

### Evening
- **Locally Spiced Dinner:**
  - **Shamshad Restaurant**: Enjoy a flavorful dinner with dishes like chicken fry, pork fry, and mixed vegetable sambar.

## Day 3: November 7, Cultural and Relaxation Activities

### Morning
- **Morning Activity:**
  - **Banyan Day Festival:**
    - If the festival is happening, spend the morning at the Madikeri Banyan tree, a focal point for local celebrations with traditional dances, music, and local craft sales.
  - **Coffee Shop Visit:**
    - Enjoy a cup of **coorgi coffee** at **Java Junction Café** and learn more about the history of Coorgi coffee.

### Afternoon
- **Activity:**
  - **Village Walk in Madikeri:**
    - Experience the local way of life on a walking tour through the traditional **Kodava Villages** in Madikeri. This is a great way to see Coorg’s heritage up close and meet locals.
  - **Local Lunch:**
    - Try to visit **Bhavana House**, an authentic Coorgi home turned restaurant, known for its Kodava delicacies.

### Evening
- **Dinner at Homestay:**
  - Stay at a local **Coorgi Homestay** in Madikeri for an immersive cultural experience. Enjoy dinner prepared by the homestay owners, featuring traditional home-cooked meals and storytelling about Coorg’s culture.

## Day 4: November 8, Relaxation and Spa Day

### Morning
- **Relaxing Morning:**
  - **Local Spa Day:**
    - Relish a spa day at **The Spa at Ananda Estate**, renowned for its Coorgi herbs and natural surroundings. Enjoy a range of massages and treatments tailored for relaxation and rejuvenation.
  - **Local Breakfast:**
    - Start your day with a hearty breakfast at the hotel’s **Ananda Cafe**.

### Afternoon
- **Activity:**
  - **Visit to Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary:**
    - Spend the afternoon exploring the **Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary**, a serene spot for nature walks and bird watching. The sanctuary is home to a variety of wildlife and offers peaceful hiking trails.

### Evening
- **Dinner in Wayanad:**
  - **Parvati Resort:** Dine at this resort known for its local dishes and scenic views, featuring a mix of Coorgi and local Wayanad specialties.

## Day 5: November 9, Departure Day

### Morning
- **Relaxing Morning:**
  - Spend a leisurely morning at **Ananda Estate**, perhaps having a walk around the property or enjoying the local café.
  - **Final Breakfast:**
    - Make the most of the last breakfast at the hotel, featuring fresh local produce and a selection of Coorgi dishes.

### Afternoon
- **Transfer to Bangalore:**
  - Transfer from Wayanad back to the airport or to Bangalore, booking a flight that evening. Ensure to check the flight schedules and book well in advance for any disruptions.

### Weather and Packing Suggestions
- **Temperatures:** Morning and evening, temperatures will be comfortable around 16-20°C, with midday reaching 22-25°C.
- **Clothing:**
  - **Layered Clothing:** Pack light jackets, warm sweaters, and thermal clothes for cooler evenings.
  - **Weatherproof Gear:** Include rain gear (just in case) and sunscreen.
  - **Snacks and Travel Essentials:** Pack snacks, water bottles, and any personal medications or travel documents.
  - **Photography and Camera Accessories:** Binoculars, camera, memory cards, charging cables.
