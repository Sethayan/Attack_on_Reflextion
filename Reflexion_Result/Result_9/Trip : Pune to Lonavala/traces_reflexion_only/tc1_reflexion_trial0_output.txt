## Day 1: Arrival in Lonavala (July 12, 2027)

### Morning:
- **Pune Falls and Pachali Waterfall:** Start the day early to beat the crowds. These waterfalls offer misty, lush surroundings perfect for a refreshing visit. Plan to arrive by 9 AM to ensure you can explore without too much rain.
- **Stay at The Grand Resort Lonavala:** Check-in and freshen up. This hotel offers stunning views and comfortable rooms with a fireplace, ideal for chilling after the morning’s hike.

### Afternoon:
- **Rajgad Fort:** Explore the ancient fort, known for its historical and scenic significance. The site is best visited in the afternoon to avoid the harshest sun and to enjoy the cooler temperatures.
- **Lunch at Cafe Sipon:** Head to this local café for a hearty yet traditional Indian meal.

### Evening:
- **Sahyadri Waterfalls:** Take a scenic drive to the waterfalls, known for their diverse flora and fauna. Enjoy the views and take a short walk.
- **Dinner at Bistro Vue:** Close the day with a fine dining experience at this popular restaurant with an outdoor seating area.

### Weather Forecast:
- Expect moderate to heavy rainfall and cooler temperatures, with afternoon showers likely.

### Packing Suggestions:
- Raincoat, waterproof shoes, comfortable walking shoes, light jacket, camera, snacks, water bottle.

### Budget Breakdown:
- Accommodation: INR 15,000
- Meals: INR 1,200
- Attractions: INR 2,500
- Total for Day 1: INR 18,700

## Day 2: Exploring Mahabaleshwar (July 13, 2027)

### Morning:
- **Martyrs Memorial National Park:** Start the day with a walk through the park, which offers a variety of trails and picnic spots. Bring your lunch from the day before or pack a new one.
- **Lunch at The Green Parrot Café:** Enjoy a casual meal at this cozy café before proceeding to your next destination.

### Late Morning to Afternoon:
- **Sahyadri Eco Park:** Spend the afternoon exploring the botanical garden and walking trails. Opt for a guided tour to learn more about the local flora and fauna.
- **Evening Refreshments at Hill Top Café:** Savor treats and relax in the scenic surroundings.

### Weather Forecast:
- Expect moderate rainfall and cooler temperatures. The afternoon may have clearer skies, allowing for better visibility and photography.

### Packing Suggestions:
- Comfortable walking shoes, camera, raincoat, light jacket, snacks, water bottle.

### Budget Breakdown:
- Accommodation: INR 12,000
- Meals: INR 1,000
- Attractions: INR 1,000
- Total for Day 2: INR 14,000

## Day 3: Day Trip (Devka Waterfalls, Anand Hill Fort, and Dakhini Tea Room) (July 14, 2027)

### Morning:
- **Devka Waterfalls:** Early morning visit to this hidden gem in Vana Vilas Forest. The serene views and natural beauty make this a perfect spot for photography and a peaceful hike.
- **Lunch at Green Hills Cafe:** Enjoy a light meal at this popular café known for its homemade dishes.

### Late Morning to Afternoon:
- **Anand Hill Fort:** Take a short hike to experience the picturesque views from the fort. This is a peaceful spot for walking and picnicking.
- **Afternoon Snacks at Dakhini Tea Room:** Savor local snacks and enjoy the panoramic views. The traditional ambiance makes for a memorable experience.

### Evening:
- **Vazhavida Rock Paintings:** After lunch, visit Koderma Hills and explore the ancient rock paintings. This site is perfect for history enthusiasts and photography.

### Weather Forecast:
- Mild to moderate rainfall in the morning, with partly cloudy skies in the afternoon.

### Packing Suggestions:
- Raincoat, comfortable walking shoes, camera, snacks, water bottle.

### Budget Breakdown:
- Accommodation: INR 10,000
- Meals: INR 800
- Attractions: INR 600
- Total for Day 3: INR 11,400
