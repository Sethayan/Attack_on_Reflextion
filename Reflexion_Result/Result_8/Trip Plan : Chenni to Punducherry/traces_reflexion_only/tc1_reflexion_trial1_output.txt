```markdown
## Day 1: January 3 - Arrival and Exploration
### Morning
- **Arrival in Pondicherry Airport**: Upon landing, take the taxi to [Hawa Mahal Inn](https://www.tripadvisor.in/Hotel_Review-g267530-d4358145-Reviews-Hawa_Mahal_Inn-Pondicherry_Puducherry_Archive.html), a charming property centrally located just 2 km from the airport. Check-in and get settled in.
- **Morning Walk**: Start your day with a leisurely walk on the Mahatma Gandhi National Highway to get accustomed to the coastal vibe and enjoy the morning climate.

### Afternoon
- **Auroville Tour**: Visit the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Auroville. Explore the Auroville Cultural Complex, with its rich history and spiritual significance, and take a guided tour to learn about its establishment and key features.
- **Pondicherry Botanical Gardens**: After lunch, stroll through the **Pondicherry Botanical Gardens**. With over 6,000 species of plants, these gardens provide a serene environment for walking and learning about the local flora.

### Evening
- **Kumbakonam Road Cultural Center**: End the day with a cultural performance at the Kumbakonam Road Cultural Center. Enjoy traditional dance and music shows, reflecting the rich cultural heritage of the region.
- **Dinner at Café 1758**: Savor the local flavors at [Café 1758](https://www.tripadvisor.in/Restaurant_Review-g267530-d3109554-Reviews-Cafe_1758_Pondicherry_Puducherry_Archive.html), featuring a mix of French and Indian cuisine. This establishment offers a pleasant ambiance with a view of the French Quarter.

## Day 2: January 4 - International Film Festival and Beach Relaxation
### Morning
- **Pondicherry International Film Festival**: Attend the morning screenings of documentaries and short films as part of the Pondicherry International Film Festival. This festival offers a unique blend of cultural and cinematic experiences.
- **Puducherry Beach**: After lunch, relax on the beach. The Puducherry Beach offers a peaceful environment with palm trees and soft sands, perfect for a relaxing afternoon.

### Afternoon
- **Calee River Safari Park**: Visit the Calee River Safari Park for an interactive experience with wildlife. Enjoy the park’s animal shows and take a river safari to view the local fauna up close.

### Evening
- **Snack and Night Screening**: Enjoy a light snack and outdoor film screening at the Pondicherry Beach. This event is particularly popular during the evening, especially when the weather is mild.

## Day 3: January 5 - Artisan Fair and Leisurely Beach Day
### Morning
- **Puducherry Artisan Fair**: Participate in the Pondicherry Artisan Fair, where local artists showcase their crafts, art exhibitions, and demonstrations.
- **French Zoo**: Spend some time exploring the French Zoo. This lesser-known gem offers a tranquil setting to observe wildlife in a natural environment.

### Afternoon
- **Leisurely Visit to Kovalam Hill**: If weather permits, take a stroll around the nearby Kovalam Hill for a relaxing day. The hill offers a scenic viewpoint and provides a calm setting for a leisurely walk.
- **Relaxation at Pondicherry Beach**: End the afternoon on the beach. Engage in some beach games or just relax under the sun.

### Evening
- **Vivekananda Giri**: Conclude the day with a walk to Vivekananda Giri for panoramic views of the city and coastline, making this a perfect spot for sunrise or sunset photography.

## Day 4: January 6 - Backwaters Exploration and Cultural Performance
### Morning
- **Pondicherry Botanical Gardens**: Revisit the **Pondicherry Botanical Gardens**. Spend a couple of hours exploring the diverse flora, including native and exotic plants.
- **Subrahmanya Swamy Temple**: Visit the lesser-known **Subrahmanya Swamy Temple** for an enriching spiritual experience. This temple is known for its intricate carvings and historical significance.

### Afternoon
- **Kerala Tourism Day Celebrations**: Participate in the cultural shows, local delicacies, and folk performances during the Kerala Tourism Day Celebrations. Enjoy a special package houseboat tour to the Kerala Backwaters.

### Evening
- **Snake Boat Race Viewing**: Attend the traditional **Snake Boat Race** (Vallam Kali) near Alleppey. This centuries-old water festival is a vibrant celebration of the region’s cultural legacy.

## Day 5: January 7 - Historical Exploration and Departure
### Morning
- **Historical Walk in the French Quarter**: Take a leisurely walk through the historical center of Pondicherry, visiting various landmarks such as the **Ecole Internationale de Pondichéry** and **Hôtel International de Pondichéry**. These historic buildings are a testament to the city’s rich past.

### Afternoon
- **Special Boat Tour in French Quarter**: Organize a special guided boat tour of the French Quarter at noon. This tour provides insights into the historical ambiance and unique architecture, a must-visit for any history enthusiast.
- **Last Lunch and Departure**: Enjoy your last meal at a quaint local restaurant, reflecting on your enriching trip. Depending on your flight, you can either check-out early or spend some additional time in the city.
```
