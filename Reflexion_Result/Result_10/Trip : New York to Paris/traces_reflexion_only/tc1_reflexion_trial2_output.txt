## Day 1: December 15, 2026 - Arrival and Adjust to the City

### Morning
- **Arrival at Charles de Gaulle Airport** (CDG)
- **Transfer to Hotel**: Ibis Styles Paris Gare de Lyon (approximately 20 minutes by train)
- **Check-in and Rest**: Spend some quiet time to adjust to the time difference.

### Afternoon
- **Visit Musée de l'Orangerie**: Explore the museum, known for its stunning artworks by Monet, including Water Lilies.
- **Dinner**: Le Comptoir du Menton, a charming bistro serving French cuisine with a focus on local and seasonal ingredients.

### Evening
- **Dinner and Bistro Experience**: Stroll through the Latin Quarter and enjoy traditional French delicacies at La Petite Parisienne.
- **Dinner Menu**: Traditional dishes like Coq au Vin, Ratatouille, and Crêpes.

### Weather: Clear to partly cloudy, temperature around 10°C (50°F).

### Packing Suggestions:
- Warm clothing (jacket, sweater, thermal layers)
- Light jacket
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Waterproof carry-on bag for light rain
- Daypack for daily excursions

### Morning
- **Louvre Museum**: Spend the morning exploring the world's largest art museum, including works like the Mona Lisa and Venus de Milo.
- **Entrance Fee**: Approx. €17, $20.

### Afternoon
- **Musée d'Orsay**: Explore the Impressionist and Post-Impressionist art collection.
- **Entrance Fee**: Free, but book your tickets online to avoid queues.

### Evening
- **Café de la Terrasse, Montmartre**: Enjoy a cozy atmosphere with homemade pastries while watching the sunset.
- **Evening Activity**: Explore the picturesque city and visit local shops.

### Weather: Chance of rain, 30%, temperature around 12°C (54°F).

- Umbrella
- Warm layer
- Camera
- Daypack
