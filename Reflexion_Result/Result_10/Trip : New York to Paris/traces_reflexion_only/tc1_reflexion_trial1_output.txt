Here is a detailed 8-day travel itinerary for Paris, ensuring a balance of historical, cultural, and leisure experiences, with estimated budgets and practical advice:

### 8-Day Paris Itinerary

#### **Day 1: Arrival and Get Acquainted**
- **Morning Arrival**: **9:00 AM - 10:00 AM** - Arrive at Charles de Gaulle Airport and check into your hotel. 
- **Afternoon Exploration**: **11:00 AM - 1:00 PM** - Walk around the Marais district, including Place des Vosges and Rue des Rosiers (for authentic street food or shopping).
- **Evening Activity**: **6:00 PM - 8:00 PM** - Dine at a local bistro, trying traditional French cuisine like escargot or coq au vin.
- **Budget**: **€100 - €150** (Hotel, food)

#### **Day 2: Historical Sites**
- **Morning Visit**: **8:30 AM - 12:00 PM** - Visit the Eiffel Tower (skip the line tickets, if needed for €65, to avoid crowds).
- **Afternoon Stroll**: **1:00 PM - 4:00 PM** - Explore the Trocadéro and its gardens, then walk down to the Seine.
- **Evening Dinner**: **6:00 PM - 8:00 PM** - Dinner at a Michelin-starred restaurant like Le Comptoir du Relais.
- **Budget**: **€150 - €200** (Eiffel Tower, food)

#### **Day 3: Arts and Culture**
- **Morning Visit**: **9:00 AM - 12:00 PM** - Visit the Musée d'Orsay for its collection of Impressionist masters.
- **Afternoon Tour**: **1:30 PM - 4:00 PM** - Explore Montmartre, visit the Sacré-Cœur Basilica, and enjoy a walk through the narrow streets.
- **Evening Activity**: **7:00 PM - 9:00 PM** - Visit Shakespeare & Company, a famous English-language bookshop in the Latin Quarter.
- **Budget**: **€100 - €150** (Museum, food, activities)

#### **Day 4: Food and Shopping**
- **Morning Experience**: **9:00 AM - 12:00 PM** - Take a food tour of Paris, tasting different regional cuisines.
- **Afternoon Visit**: **1:30 PM - 4:00 PM** - Head to the Marché des Enfants Rouges for gourmet food and souvenirs.
- **Evening Dinner**: **6:00 PM - 8:00 PM** - Dinner at a cozy bistro like Le Jules Verne for a scenic view of the Eiffel Tower.
- **Budget**: **€130 - €180** (Food tours, food, food)

#### **Day 5: Architecture and Parks**
- **Morning Visit**: **9:00 AM - 12:00 PM** - Walk through the Louvre Palace and its Cour Carrée.
- **Afternoon Lunch**: **1:00 PM - 2:30 PM** - Enjoy a leisurely lunch in the Jardin des Tuileries.
- **Evening Event**: **7:00 PM - 8:00 PM** - Sunset at the Jardin du Luxembourg. Evening performances are often held here.
- **Budget**: **€75 - €125** (Museum, food, activities)

#### **Day 6: Museums and Entertainment**
- **Morning Visit**: **9:00 AM - 12:00 PM** - Visit the Centre Pompidou for modern and contemporary art. Consider guided tours for insights.
- **Afternoon Visit**: **1:30 PM - 4:00 PM** - Visit the Musée de l'Orangerie, part of the Rodin Museum.
- **Evening Performance**: **7:00 PM - 9:00 PM** - Enjoy a show such as the Magic Planet, where you can see stunning illusions.
- **Budget**: **€200 - €250** (Museums, food, show)

#### **Day 7: History and Technology**
- **Morning Visit**: **9:00 AM - 12:00 PM** - Tour the Palace of Versailles from the outskirts. Arrange transportation (bus or train) from Paris. (One-way train: €5.40).
- **Afternoon Visit**: **1:30 PM - 4:00 PM** - Explore the Invalides, home to the Dôme des Invalides and Napoleon’s tomb.
- **Evening Stroll**: **7:00 PM - 8:00 PM** - Enjoy a night tour of the Sainte-Chapelle.
- **Budget**: **€200 - €250** (Versailles, food, transportation, show)

#### **Day 8: Relax and Departure**
- **Morning Activity**: **9:00 AM - 12:00 PM** - Take a guided boat tour along the Seine or a ride on the Seine River for a tranquil view.
- **Afternoon Visit**: **1:30 PM - 4:00 PM** - Spend some time in Luxembourg Gardens for a peaceful walk.
- **Evening Departure**: **5:00 PM - 7:00 PM** - Prepare for your departure back home.
- **Budget**: **€100 - €150** (Boat tour, transportation, exit fees)

### **Budget Summary:**
- **Accommodation**: **€800 - €1,200** (assuming a mid-range hotel)
- **Transportation**: **€300 - €500** (metro, trains, buses, and taxis)
- **Activities and Sightseeing**: **€750 - €1,000** for all tickets and activities
- **Food**: **€400 - €600** for dining and street food
- **Miscellaneous Expenses**: **€200 - €400** for souvenirs, tips, and last-minute dinners

**Total Estimated Budget**: **€2,850 - €4,400**

This itinerary ensures a diverse and enriching experience, with each day providing unique historical, cultural, and leisure experiences. Adjust the schedule and budget based on personal interests and preferences.
