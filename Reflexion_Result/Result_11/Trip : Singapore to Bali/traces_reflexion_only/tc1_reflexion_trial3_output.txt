## Day 1: Arrival in Bali (September 8, 2026)
- **Morning**: Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport and transfer to your accommodation in Seminyak.
- **Afternoon**: Check into your accommodation in Seminyak, located at The Legian Seminyak, Bali. This hotel offers a perfect blend of relaxation and convenient proximity to the beach.
- **Evening**: Stroll along the beach at Jimbaran Bay, enjoying the breathtaking sunset. For dinner, visit the nearby restaurant [Jimbaran Bay Seafood Restaurant](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g2979608-d10600371-Reviews-Jimbaran_Bay_Seafood_Restaurant-Jimbaran_Beach_Area_Seminyak_South_Bali_Seminyak_Bali.html), known for its fresh seafood.
- **Packing Suggestions**: Pack light, casual clothing for the beach and evening. Consider a light jacket as the evenings can get slightly cooler. Bring a beach bag, sunscreen, and swimwear.

## Day 2: Beach and Cultural Day
- **Morning**: Engage in water sports such as surfing or snorkeling at Seminyak Beach.
- **Afternoon**: Explore Seminyak Village and visit [Seminyak Art Auction Centre](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionReview-g2979608-d4592636-Reviews-Seminyak_Art_Auction_Centre-Seminyak.html) for unique shopping experiences.
- **Evening**: Sample the local street food at [Uma Uma Nasi Goreng](https://www.tripadvisor.com/RestaurantReview-g2979608-d10601368-Reviews-Uma_Uma_Nasi_Goreng-Seminyak_Bali.html).
- **Packing Suggestions**: Pack swimwear, sunglasses, hat, and sunscreen. Bring a camera to capture the scenic views.

## Day 3: Temple and Cultural Exploration
- **Morning**: Visit the Taman Ayun Temple in Ubud, known for its picturesque beauty.
- **Afternoon**: Explore the traditional market of [Museum of Bali](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionReview-g2979071-d10145617-Reviews-Museum_of_Bali-Ubud_Bali.html) to learn about Balinese culture.
- **Evening**: Enjoy traditional Balinese dance performances at [Narmada Balinese Dance](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g2979071-d10313603-Reviews-Narmada_Balinese_Dance-Ubud_Bali.html).
- **Packing Suggestions**: Pack comfortable clothing suitable for ceremonies and traditional dress, if desired.

## Day 4: Adventure Day
- **Morning**: Head to the cliffs of [Pura Luhur Uluwatu Temple](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionReview-g2979670-d10192664-Reviews-Pura_Luhur_Uluwatu_Temple-Uluwatu.html) for breathtaking views.
- **Afternoon**: Engage in yoga or meditation sessions at [Green School Bali](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionReview-g2979608-d10602268-Reviews-Green_School_Bali-Seminyak.html).
- **Evening**: Relax by the beach at Padangbai or return to Seminyak for dinner at [Buburas Jimbaran](https://www.tripadvisor.com/RestaurantReview-g2979608-d4794612-Reviews-Buburas_Jimbaran-Seminyak_Bali.html).
- **Packing Suggestions**: Pack comfortable clothing for yoga, sunscreen, and a beach bag.

## Day 5: Wellness and Cultural Experience
- **Morning**: Engage in a rice field farming activity in [Bali Agri Farm](https://www.baliagrifarm.com/).
- **Afternoon**: Relax in the spa and enjoy a traditional massage at [Bali Wellness Center](https://www.baliwellnesscenter.com/).
- **Evening**: Enjoy a traditional Balinese dinner at [Taman Nusa Ubud](https://www.tripadvisor.com/RestaurantReview-g2979071-d10313672-Reviews-Taman_Nusa_Ubud-Ubud_Bali.html).
- **Packing Suggestions**: Pack comfortable clothing for yoga and a beach bag.

## Day 6: Backcountry Adventure
- **Morning**: Explore the rural backcountry, visiting smaller villages and hidden temples like [Pura Taman Saraswati](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionReview-g2979670-d10190689-Reviews-Pura_Taman_Saraswati-Uluwatu.html).
- **Afternoon**: Visit the [Kecak Dance Performance](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g2979670-d10190690-Reviews-Kecak_Dance_Production-Uluwatu_Culture_Village.html) in Pura Luhur Uluwatu Temple.
- **Evening**: Reflect and relax in the serene surroundings.
- **Packing Suggestions**: Pack layered clothing for cooler evenings and comfortable shoes for walking.

## Day 7: Cultural and Beach Bliss
- **Morning**: Visit the stunning Pura Besakih Temple for an early morning ceremony.
- **Afternoon**: Relax at Padangbai Beach or visit the [Kuta Water Sports Center](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionReview-g2979670-d10239693-Reviews-Kuta_Water_Sports_Center-Uluwatu_Bali.html) for some water activities.
- **Evening**: Sample the vibrant street food scene in [Padangbai Market](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionReview-g2979670-d10307217-Reviews-Padangbai_Market-Uluwatu_Bali.html).
- **Packing Suggestions**: Pack light, casual clothing for the beach, sunscreen, and a camera.

## Budget Breakdown
### Accommodation
- Mid-range hotel: ~2,500-3,000 IDR ($77-$93 USD) per night

### Meals
- Simple meal: 50-100 IDR ($1.65-$3.30 USD)

### Transportation
- Tuk-tuk: 20-30 IDR ($0.65-$1 USD)

### Total Estimated Budget
- **Daily Expenses**: Approximately 10,000-12,000 IDR ($30-$37 USD) per day
- **Total for 7 Days**: $210-$260 USD

---

Bali offers a truly unique and enriching travel experience. With a mix of beautiful beaches, rich cultural heritage, and delicious street food, you'll have a memorable trip. Safe travels!
