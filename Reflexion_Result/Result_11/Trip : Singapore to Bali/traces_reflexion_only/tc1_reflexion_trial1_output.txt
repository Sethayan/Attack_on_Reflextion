- **Estimated time**: 09:00 AM – 11:00 AM

- **Afternoon activities**:

  - **Lunch**:

### Evening
**Activity: Street Food in Sanur**
- **Afternoon activities**:
  - **Visit**: Sanur Street to explore local street food vendors and restaurants.
  - **Dinner**:
    - **Suggestion**: Warung Sate for traditional Balinese grilled meat skewers.
  - **Time**: 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM

### Weather Conditions
- **Day 8**:
  - **Temperature**: 24°C to 31°C

- **Evening**: Comfortable and casual clothing for street food and dinner.

### Budget Breakdown
