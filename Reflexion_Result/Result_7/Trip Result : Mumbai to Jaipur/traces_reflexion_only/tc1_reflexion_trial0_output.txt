## Day 1: December 20, Arrival and Exploration
### Morning
- **Activity**: Transfer from Jaipur Lal Bhag Airport to your designated hotel.
- **Venue**: **Royal Jaipur Hotel** - Check-in and rest in the hotel.
- **Meal**: Breakfast at the hotel’s restaurant, **Royal Jaipur Cafe**.

### Afternoon
- **Activity**: Explore the **City Palace** and **Amber Fort**.
- **Venue**: **City Palace** and **Amber Fort**.
- **Meal**: Lunch at **Sakina’s Cafe**, known for local Rajasthani cuisine.

### Evening
- **Activity**: Enjoy an **evening walk** around the **pink city**, experiencing the vibrant **Jantar Mantar**.
- **Venue**: **Jantar Mantar**.
- **Meal**: Dinner at **Mughal Times**, a popular restaurant for Indian and Mughlai dishes.

### Packing Suggestions
- **Clothing**: Layers for chilly evenings, breathable fabrics for daytime, comfortable walking shoes.
- **Personal Documents**: Passport, visa, vaccination records, local currency, and any necessary travel insurance.
- **Electronics**: Phone, charger, power bank, camera or camera phone.

### Budget Breakdown
- **Average Accommodation Cost**: INR 5,000 - INR 7,500 per night, depending on the hotel chosen.
- **Flights**: INR 3,000 - INR 6,000 per person.
- **Daily Local Transport**: INR 200 - INR 300 per day.
- **Food/Meals**: INR 2,000 - INR 3,000 per day.
- **Entrance Fees to Attractions**: INR 1,000 - INR 2,000 per day.
- **Total Estimated Cost**: INR 21,000 - INR 39,000 for a 7-night/8-day trip.
