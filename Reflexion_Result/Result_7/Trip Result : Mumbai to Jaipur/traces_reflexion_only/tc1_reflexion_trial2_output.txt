## Day 1: December 20, Arrival and City Exploration
### Morning
- **Activity**: **Visit Hawa Mahal**
  - Arrive at Jaipur and start with a visit to the Hawa Mahal, known for its unique five-story pyramid structure. Enjoy stunning views and intricate latticework.
- **Times**: 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM
- **Packing**: Comfortable walking shoes, sunglasses, and a light jacket for the sun.

### Afternoon
- **Activity**: **Explore City Palace Museum and Garden**
  - Head to the City Palace Museum for a detailed look at the palace's opulent interiors and beautiful gardens. Consider booking a guided tour for a deeper understanding.
- **Packing**: Sunscreen, hats, and a camera.
- **Food Suggestion**: **Loco Moco** at **The Art House**

### Evening
- **Activity**: **Dinner at Mughal-e-Azam Restaurant**
  - Experience traditional Rajasthani cuisine with a formal evening meal at Mughal-e-Azam.
- **Packing**: Comfortable clothing and a nice outfit due to formal dinner.
- **Weather Forecast**: 22°C during the day, 8°C at night. Pack layers.

## Day 2: December 21, Historical Forts and Palaces
### Morning
- **Activity**: **Visit Amber Fort**
  - Begin the day by ascending the hill to explore Amber Fort, known for its Diwan-i-Aam and intricate architecture. Book a local guide if needed.
- **Times**: 9:00 AM to 10:30 AM
- **Packing**: Water, sunscreen, and comfortable walking shoes.

### Afternoon
- **Activity**: **Jaigarh Fort Tour**
  - Spend the afternoon at Jaigarh Fort to explore its well-maintained structures and the famous Jaivana cannon, the largest cannon in the world.
- **Times**: 11:30 AM to 12:30 PM
- **Packing**: Snacks and a light jacket for mild evening temperatures.

### Evening
- **Activity**: **Evening Walk in Ram Niwas Garden**
  - End the day with a relaxing evening walk through Ram Niwas Garden, a beautiful oasis in the heart of the city.
- **Times**: 5:00 PM to 6:30 PM
- **Packing**: Comfortable shoes and a camera for photos.

- **Packing**: Comfortable walking shoes and a camera.

### Afternoon
- **Activity**: **Sisodia Rani Ka Bagh**
  - Visit the historic Sisodia Rani Ka Bagh, featuring a blend of traditional culture and modern art.
- **Times**: 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM
- **Packing**: Sunscreen, hats, and a camera for beautiful scenery.

### Evening
- **Activity**: **Dinner at Ghewar Speciality Restaurant**
  - Enjoy a delicious evening meal with sweet snacks like Ghewar, a traditional Rajasthani treat.
- **Packing**: Comfortable clothing and a light jacket.
- **Weather Forecast**: 21°C during the day, 7°C at night. Pack layers.
