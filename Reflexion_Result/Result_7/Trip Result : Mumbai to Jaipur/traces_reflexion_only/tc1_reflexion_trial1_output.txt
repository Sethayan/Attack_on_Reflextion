## Day 1: December 20 - Arrival and Exploration of Jaipur

### Morning
- **Activity:** Arrive in Jaipur and get settled at The Alsisar Hotel
  - **Hotel:** The Alsisar is a 3-star hotel located close to the city center. It offers comfortable accommodations and is conveniently situated near major attractions.
- **Morning Walk:** Begin your day with a visit to the iconic Hawa Mahal. Arrive early to beat the crowds and enjoy the vibrant colors and intricate architecture.
  
### Afternoon
- **Activity:** Explore the City Palace
  - **Venue:** City Palace, located adjacent to Hawa Mahal. Guided tours can be booked in advance.
  - **Time:** Afternoon (around 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM)
  - **Description:** The City Palace is a must-visit for its historical and cultural significance. This grand complex houses numerous museums and galleries that provide insights into the city's rich history.

- **Dining Experience:** Enjoy lunch at The Banyan
  - **Restaurant:** A fusion café that offers a mix of traditional and modern cuisine. It’s a nice place to take a break and grab a mid-day meal.

### Evening
- **Activity:** Sunset at Sajjan Pol
  - **Venue:** Sajjan Pol, located on the City Palace grounds. Take a leisurely stroll and watch the sunset from this stunning arch.
  - **Time:** 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM
  - **Description:** The sunset at Sajjan Pol is a memorable experience. It offers a panoramic view of the Aravalli Hills in the distance.

### Packing Suggestions
- **Day 1:** Comfortable walking shoes, light clothing, and rain gear (just in case).

### Budget Breakdown
- **Accommodation:** INR 5,000 (The Alsisar Hotel for one night)
- **Lunch:** INR 1,000
- **Ticket and Tour:** INR 2,000 (Hawa Mahal and City Palace)
- **Miscellaneous:** INR 500

## Day 2: December 21 - Fort and Heritage Exploration

### Morning
- **Activity:** Visit Amber Fort
  - **Venue:** One of the most popular fort complexes in Jaipur. Early morning is the best time to visit to avoid the heat and crowds.
  - **Time:** 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM
  - **Description:** Amber Fort is a blend of Mughal and Rajput architecture and offers stunning views of the surrounding landscape.

- **Activity:** Boat Ride on Lake Pichola
  - **Venue:** Start the boat ride from Pichola Island. You can end the day with a peaceful sunset at Jal Mahal.
  - **Boat Ride Time:** 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM
  - **Description:** This is a fantastic way to take in the beautiful views of Jaipur from the water.

### Afternoon
- **Activity:** Explore Nahargarh Fort
  - **Venue:** Located on a hill offering panoramic views of the city. Visit in the afternoon for a panoramic view and fresh air.
  - **Time:** 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM
  - **Description:** The fort is perfect for a relaxing walk to take in the views, and it’s less crowded in the afternoon.

- **Dining Experience:** Lunch at Gujarat Bhojanalaya
  - **Restaurant:** Authentic Rajasthani cuisine with a beautiful ambiance. Enjoy a traditional Thali lunch.
  - **Time:** 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM

### Evening
- **Activity:** Explore Bapu Bazaar
  - **Venue:** An old market selling handcrafted items, textiles, and jewelry.
  - **Time:** 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM
  - **Description:** Bapu Bazaar is bustling with activity in the evening and offers a variety of shopping opportunities.

### Packing Suggestions
- **Day 2:** Comfortable walking shoes, light clothing, and a camera for photography.

### Budget Breakdown
- **Accommodation:** INR 5,000 (The Alsisar Hotel for one night)
- **Dining Experience:** INR 800
- **Boat Ride and Fort Entrance Fees:** INR 2,000
- **Miscellaneous:** INR 200

## Day 5: December 24 - Exploring Sanganer

### Morning
- **Activity:** Visit Sanganer Textile Mills
  - **Venue:** A hub for traditional block printing and other textile crafts.
  - **Time:** 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM
  - **Description:** Get a behind-the-scenes look at the block printing process and learn the traditional craft.

### Afternoon
- **Activity:** Visit The Weaver's Studio
  - **Venue:** A small but exquisite studio where artisans continue the traditional craft of weaving.
  - **Time:** 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM
  - **Description:** Purchase unique, handcrafted items directly from the weavers.

- **Dining Experience:** Lunch at Chokhi Dhani
